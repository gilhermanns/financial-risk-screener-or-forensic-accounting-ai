# Limitations & Scope

This system is designed as a screening and prioritization tool, not a full diligence engine.

## Key Limitations

- **No Valuation Logic**: The tool does not perform DCF, LBO, or multiples-based valuation.
- **Off-Balance-Sheet Risks**: It cannot detect risks not disclosed in the provided financial statements or text notes.
- **Formula Integrity**: It assumes the input data is correct and does not audit Excel formula integrity.
- **NLP Scope**: The NLP engine relies on sophisticated pattern detection and negation handling, but it does not possess full semantic understanding of complex legal or financial nuances.

## Intended Use

Use this tool to:
- Triage large volumes of potential investment targets.
- Identify specific areas where analyst time should be focused during deep-dive diligence.
- Support professional judgment with data-driven risk signals.
